# "The Night Is Mine" — 希腊暗黑合成器二人组 INCIRRINA 以 "Trace" 寻找逃离反乌托邦的庇护

**来源：** Post-Punk.com
**日期：** 2026-06-16
**链接：** https://post-punk.com/the-night-is-mine-greek-dark-synth-duo-incirrina-finds-shelter-from-dystopian-conformity-with-trace/

**专辑：** Trace
**艺人：** Incirrina

---

*你等到今夜*

*轨迹的偏离*

*准备好迎接吸血时刻*

*舞台，另一副面孔*

Some things are monuments: raised in stone, fixed in place, built to instruct the future on what must be remembered. A trace is different. It is smaller, stranger, more unstable: a smear of light on glass, a footprint in dust, a pulse still moving beneath the surface.

有些东西是纪念碑：由石头竖立，固定于原地，为告诉未来什么必须被铭记而建。痕迹不是这样的。它更小、更奇怪、更不稳定：玻璃上的一抹光痕，尘土中的一个脚印，表面下仍在跳动的脉搏。

A trace does not announce itself from above. It survives at the edge of disappearance, carrying proof that something passed through, resisted, or remained.

痕迹不会自上而下地宣告自己的存在。它存活着于消失的边缘，承载着某物曾经过、曾抵抗或曾残留的证明。

On their third full-length album, *Trace*, Athens dark synth duo Incirrina follow those marks through the maze of the "seeming real," where what is imposed, cruel, and inhumane presents itself as inevitable truth.

在第三张全长专辑 *Trace* 中，来自雅典的暗黑合成器二人组 Incirrina 追随着这些标记，穿过"看似真实"的迷宫——在那里，强加的、残酷的、不人道的被呈现为不可回避的真相。

Here, the night is not merely a setting. It is a stage, a shelter, a ritual chamber, and a corridor of escape.

在这里，夜晚不仅仅是一个场景。它是一个舞台、一个庇护所、一个仪式空间，也是一条逃离的通道。

Cold analogue electronics flicker like neon over wet stone; basslines gallop and coil; drum machines strike with martial insistence; voices rise from the fog like warnings, spells, accusations, and prayers.

冰冷的模拟电子乐像霓虹灯一样在湿石上闪烁；贝斯线奔腾蜷缩；鼓机以军令般的坚决击打；人声从雾中升起，如同警告、咒语、指控和祈祷。

*Trace* is a record of movement, but also of refusal: a dance through dread, memory, alienation, and defiance.

*Trace* 是一张关于运动的专辑，也是关于拒绝的专辑：一场穿越恐惧、记忆、疏离与反抗的舞蹈。

With *Trace*, George Katsanos and Irini Tiniakou expand the distinctive sonic universe they established with *Lip Led Scream* in 2022 and *8.15* in 2019, experimenting with instrumentation, rhythm, and vocal approach.

凭借 *Trace*，George Katsanos 和 Irini Tiniakou 扩展了他们通过 2022 年的 *Lip Led Scream* 和 2019 年的 *8.15* 建立起来的独特声学宇宙，在器乐编配、节奏和人声表达上进行实验。

The album gathers 11 songs written between 2023 and 2025 and shaped through the discipline of a 24-channel console, exploring the boundaries of dark electronic, synthwave, coldwave, minimal synth, and darkwave without reducing itself to the tropes of any of these genres.

专辑汇集了 2023 至 2025 年间创作的 11 首歌曲，在 24 通道调音台的严格塑造下，探索暗黑电子、合成器浪潮、冷波、极简合成器和暗潮的边界，却不沦陷于其中任何一个流派的陈规。

Thematically, the songs explore the uniqueness of human experience and the struggle to preserve inner freedom and individuality in dystopian times.

在主题上，歌曲探索人类经验的独特性，以及反乌托邦时代中保持内心自由与个性的挣扎。

Celebrating diversity and self-expression while continuing to connect, create, and work with others becomes a way out of the false order.

在继续与他人联结、创造与合作的同时，颂扬多样性和自我表达，成为逃离虚假秩序的出路。

The album itself is the result of such a process: its creators consider themselves, against the odds, artisans and friends who make music, share it, and reinvent themselves.

专辑本身就是这样一种过程的产物：创作者们不顾困难，自认为是制作音乐、分享音乐并不断重塑自我的工匠和朋友。

As its title indicates, *Trace* is the imprint of searching for wondrous, meaningful diversions, novel transformations, and transitory acts of care and defiance.

正如其名所示，*Trace* 是追寻奇妙而有意义的偏离、新颖的蜕变，以及短暂但充满关怀与反抗的行动所留下的印记。

Katsanos brings analogue synthesizers, octapad, drum programming, electric bass guitar, kantele, alto melodica, samples, waterphone, and vocals into Incirrina's sonic architecture, while Tiniakou's synthesizers and unmistakable voice give the duo much of its spectral charge.

Katsanos 将模拟合成器、八音垫、鼓机编程、电贝斯、坎特莱琴、中音口风琴、采样、水琴和人声引入 Incirrina 的声音建筑，而 Tiniakou 的合成器和不可替代的声音赋予了这个二人组大部分的超自然质感。

Her delivery can feel whispered, wounded, commanding, or ritualistic, cutting through the electronic framework with theatrical force.

她的演绎可以是耳语般的、受伤的、威严的或仪式性的，以戏剧性的力量穿透电子音乐的框架。

On *Trace*, that chemistry is central: machinery and voice, pulse and poetry, body and alarm.

在 *Trace* 中，这种化学反应是核心：机械与人声，脉搏与诗歌，身体与警报。

"Mutate" opens the album with drones that sound like metal being cut, before a galloping bass synth melody pushes the track forward with immediate force.

"Mutate" 以听起来像金属切割的持续低音开启专辑，随后奔腾的合成器贝斯旋律以即刻的力量推动整首歌曲前行。

Katsanos' analogue electronics and drum programming give the song its relentless propulsion, while Tiniakou's voice begins close, dark, and breathy before sharpening into an old-school goth edge.

Katsanos 的模拟电子乐和鼓机编程赋予歌曲 relentless 的推进力，而 Tiniakou 的声音起初是近距离的、黑暗的、带着气声的，随后锐化为一种老派哥特的锋芒。

Buzzing synth sighs gather around the pulse, and when the drums lock into a dance beat, the track surges with classic 80s synthpop and darkwave energy.

嗡鸣的合成器叹息汇聚在脉搏周围，当鼓机锁定舞曲节拍时，整首曲目迸发出经典的 80 年代合成器流行和暗潮能量。

It churns, glows, and moves with the confidence of an opening manifesto.

它翻涌、发光、前行，带着开场宣言般的自信。

Through its lyrics, "Mutate" frames the night as a ritual of self-remaking, where performance, fashion, fire, and the dance floor become weapons against conformity.

通过歌词，"Mutate" 将夜晚构建为一场自我重塑的仪式，表演、时尚、火焰和舞池成为对抗从众的武器。

The lyrics reject fixed identity, social obedience, faceless control, and dull authority, choosing eruption over submission, imagination over instruction.

歌词拒绝固定的身份、社会的服从、无面孔的控制和沉闷的权威，选择爆发而非顺从，选择想象而非说教。
