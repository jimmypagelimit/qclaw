# Olivia Rodrigo 第三张专辑：成熟与自律

**来源：** PopMatters
**日期：** 2026-06-16
**链接：** https://www.popmatters.com/olivia-rodrigo-you-seem-pretty

**专辑：** You Seem Pretty Sad For a Girl So in Love
**艺人：** Olivia Rodrigo
**厂牌：** Geffen
**发行：** 2026 年 6 月 12 日

---

"I lead a very private life, sort of," Olivia Rodrigo told British Vogue earlier this year, "that makes me feel protected enough to be able to bare my soul in the songwriting."

"我过着一个相当私密的生活，" Olivia Rodrigo 今年早些时候告诉英国《Vogue》，"这让我感到足够安全，能够在创作歌曲时坦露灵魂。"

With the release of her third studio album, *You Seem Pretty Sad For a Girl So in Love*, a concept record following the course of a relationship, the singer has become as confessional as ever, eschewing flashy pop-punk hooks for depth and maturity.

随着第三张录音室专辑 *You Seem Pretty Sad For a Girl So in Love* 的发行——一张追踪一段感情全过程的概念专辑——这位歌手变得比以往更加坦白自白，她放弃了花哨的流行朋克 hook，转而追求深度和成熟。

"I'm a sad shell of a woman, and I've got maggots for brains," Rodrigo sings, all because she feels empty that her lover has gone away.

"我是个悲伤的女人躯壳，脑子里全是蛆虫，" Rodrigo 唱道，仅仅因为爱人离去而感到空虚。

While the singer has always crafted her songs in a campy fashion that leaves her figuratively smiling and winking at her audience, Rodrigo takes this skill to new heights on her latest LP.

尽管这位歌手一直以一种故作夸张的方式创作歌曲，仿佛在对听众微笑和眨眼，但 Rodrigo 在这张新专辑中将这种技艺推向了新高度。

On her previous album *Guts*, Rodrigo proclaimed her ability to both know her age and act like it, a premise she both continues and grows beyond on *You Seem Pretty Sad For a Girl So in Love*.

在上一张专辑 *Guts* 中，Rodrigo 宣称自己既了解自己的年龄，也表现得符合这个年龄——这个前提在 *You Seem Pretty Sad For a Girl So in Love* 中得到了延续，也超越了它。

While no one is suddenly expecting any different from her, there has always been something uniquely and subtly powerful about Rodrigo's work in that it appears she is being entirely herself all of the time, which isn't something every pop star can necessarily say.

虽然没人突然对她有什么不同期待，但 Rodrigo 的作品中始终有一种独特而微妙的强大力量：她似乎每时每刻都在做完全的自己——这不是每个流行明星都能说的。

As much as she is massively popular within her own age demographic, Rodrigo has amassed a large fanbase with listeners of all ages, particularly those who were teenagers when the singer was born.

尽管 Rodrigo 在同龄人中极受欢迎，但她也积累了各年龄段的大量粉丝，尤其是那些在她出生时还是青少年的人。

Two decades ago, Rodrigo would have been mocked and derided for putting her entire self on display. Now, female listeners who lived through the ugly early aughts applaud her poetic sense of coming of age in real time, and rightfully so.

二十年前，Rodrigo 会因为将完整的自我展示出来而遭到嘲笑和贬损。如今，经历过丑陋的千禧年初的女性听众们为她在实时成长中的诗意而鼓掌，这是理所应当的。

However, Rodrigo is older than she was when she first broke through in pop music some five years ago. While the undercurrent of her work has always been melancholy, the singer is more cynical now than on her last album, which only further sharpens her edge as a young female musician.

然而，Rodrigo 比五年前刚在流行乐坛崭露头角时更成熟了。虽然她作品的底色一直是忧郁，但比起上一张专辑，她现在更加愤世嫉俗，这进一步磨砺了她作为年轻女音乐家的锋芒。

"I feel right, I feel wrong, I feel totally insane / And I want you more than any stupid song could ever say," she sings on "Stupid Song", which is just further proof that Rodrigo isn't about to make herself more palatable to express herself.

"我感觉对，我感觉错，我感觉彻底疯了 / 我对你的渴望比任何愚蠢的歌曲所能表达的都更深，" 她在 "Stupid Song" 中唱道——这进一步证明 Rodrigo 不打算为了迎合他人而克制自己的表达。

Elsewhere, she comes to conclusions about herself that she can easily make at 23 that she wouldn't have been able to make at 20. "It's a small world / When it only can revolve around us two," Rodrigo reasons on "Purple", one of the record's most confessional offerings. "It's crazy / I had big dreams 'til I tied myself to you / Now I'm all-consumed."

在其他地方，她得出了 23 岁才能轻易得出、20 岁时不可能意识到的关于自己的结论。"世界很小 / 当它只能围绕我们两个旋转，" Rodrigo 在专辑中最自白式的歌曲之一 "Purple" 中说。"这很疯狂 / 我曾经有远大的梦想，直到把自己绑在你身上 / 现在我已完全被吞噬。"

While young Gen Z women may now be more likely to reach these realizations about themselves and their relationships earlier in life, lyrics like these also resonate with older female listeners from generations in which empowerment was scarce.

虽然 Z 世代年轻女性现在可能更早地意识到关于自己和关系的这些道理，但这样的歌词也能与那些成长于女性赋权稀缺年代的大龄女性听众产生共鸣。

*You Seem Pretty Sad For a Girl So in Love* also marks the first guest appearance on an Olivia Rodrigo album, and it's not from one of her mentors or fellow confessional female singer-songwriters.

*You Seem Pretty Sad For a Girl So in Love* 也是 Olivia Rodrigo 专辑中首次出现嘉宾演唱，而且不是来自她的导师或其他自白式女创作歌手。

Indeed, Robert Smith of the Cure contributing vocals to a Gen Z pop star's album seems like the year's biggest musical fever dream, yet it works flawlessly.

确实，The Cure 的 Robert Smith 为一位 Z 世代流行明星的专辑献声，听起来像是今年最大的音乐狂梦，但它完美地奏效了。

In "What's Wrong With Me", they sing: "My head is spinning, and my stomach is sick / Say I'm in love, so it's hard to admit / I can't eat, I can't sleep / I think you're what's wrong with me."

在 "What's Wrong With Me" 中，他们唱道："我头晕目眩，胃里翻江倒海 / 说我在恋爱中，所以难以承认 / 我吃不下，睡不好 / 我想你就是我问题的所在。"

As Rodrigo effortlessly describes the notion of becoming physically ill rather than being stuck in someone's orbit, Smith's presence only adds to the glory of both the songwriting and the current cultural moment.

Rodrigo 轻松地描述了因陷入某人的引力轨道而真正生病的感受，而 Smith 的存在只会为歌曲创作和当前的文化时刻增添光辉。
