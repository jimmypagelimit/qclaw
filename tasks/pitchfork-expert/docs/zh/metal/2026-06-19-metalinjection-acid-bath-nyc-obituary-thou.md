# ACID BATH 将与 OBITUARY 和 THOU 在纽约同台

**来源：** Metal Injection | **日期：** 2026-06-19

---

Acid Bath have added a new date to their upcoming U.S. tour, seeing them hit the Rooftop At Pier 17 in New York City, NY on August 21 with Obituary and Thou.

第1段：Acid Bath为他们即将到来的美国巡演新增了一站——8月21日将在纽约市Pier 17屋顶剧场与Obituary和Thou同台演出。

---

Pre-sale tickets for the show are currently on sale using the code "MUTATE," with general on-sale tickets launching tomorrow, June 18, at 10 a.m. EST.

第2段：该演出的预售票目前正在发售，使用代码"MUTATE"，普通售票将于明天6月18日上午10点（美东时间）开始。

---

After a stop in Manchester, UK next week, Acid Bath will make other European stops with System Of A Down and Queens Of The Stone Age. They'll come back to the states in August for several headlining dates.

第3段：下周在曼彻斯特停留后，Acid Bath将与System Of A Down和Queens Of The Stone Age一起进行欧洲巡演。他们将于8月返回美国进行多场头条演出。

---

Tour highlights include stops with Russian Circles, YOB, Melvins, Kylesa, Ministry, Windhand, and more across various dates.

第4段：巡演亮点包括在不同日期与Russian Circles、YOB、Melvins、Kylesa、Ministry、Windhand等乐队同台。
