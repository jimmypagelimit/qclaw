# THE DILLINGER ESCAPE PLAN 公布《Calculating Infinity》25周年巡演最后一批场次

**来源：** Metal Injection | **日期：** 2026-06-19

---

Earlier this month, The Dillinger Escape Plan teased the idea that their nearly two-year-long celebratory tour for the 25th anniversary of their 1999 debut record Calculating Infinity was coming to an end. Now, they've officially shared the final five dates for the tour, all to occur before they embark on the Headbanger's Boat.

第1段：本月早些时候，The Dillinger Escape Plan暗示他们为1999年首张专辑《Calculating Infinity》举办的近两年25周年庆祝巡演即将结束。现在，他们正式公布了巡演的最后五场演出，全部在他们登上Headbanger's Boat金属邮轮之前进行。

---

They'll be hitting Baltimore, Portland, Vancouver, Seattle, and St. Petersberg before taking off in Miami for the metal cruise. Along for the last round of dates will be Johnny Booth, XCOMM, Squid Pisser, Many Eyes, Frontierer and more on various stops.

第2段：他们将在巴尔的摩、波特兰、温哥华、西雅图和圣彼得堡演出，然后从迈阿密出发登上金属邮轮。最后这轮演出的各站嘉宾包括Johnny Booth、XCOMM、Squid Pisser、Many Eyes、Frontierer等。

---

Tickets for the final Calculating Infinity go on sale this Friday, June 19, at 10:00am local time.

第3段：《Calculating Infinity》最后巡演的门票将于本周五6月19日上午10点（当地时间）开售。
