# INSOMNIUM 发布新曲"Shadowlife"MV

**来源：** Metal Injection | **日期：** 2026-06-19

---

Finnish melodic death metal band Insomnium have shared the new video for their single "Shadowlife," the first to come off their upcoming 10th record set to release this October.

第1段：芬兰旋律死亡金属乐队Insomnium发布了新单曲"Shadowlife"的MV，这是他们即将于今年十月发行的第十张专辑中的首发曲目。

---

According to Insomnium vocalist/bassist Niilo Sevänen, "Shadowlife" is about that what can remain once darkness takes cover.

第2段：据Insomnium主唱/贝斯手Niilo Sevänen介绍，"Shadowlife"讲述的是当黑暗褪去后，还能剩下什么。

---

"As a story, 'Shadowlife' can be seen as a thematic gateway to the entire record — an elegy not only for a lost beloved, but also for the fading belief that anything beautiful can remain untouched by time," says Sevänen. "It is a realization that love, youth, summer, and warmth cannot be preserved forever. Thus, we are left suspended between longing and acceptance, lingering in the liminal spaces of sleep, shadow, and night."

第3段："作为一个故事，'Shadowlife'可以被视为整张专辑的主题入口——不仅是一首献给逝去之人的挽歌，也是对美好事物终将被时间侵蚀这一信念消退的哀悼，"Sevänen说。"这是一种领悟：爱、青春、夏天和温暖无法永远保存。于是我们被悬置在渴望与接受之间，徘徊在睡眠、阴影和黑夜的阈限空间里。"

---

To boot, "Shadowlife" also marks somewhat of a turning point for the band's sound, though still maintaining the heaviness that's kept them going for nine records strong.

第4段：此外，"Shadowlife"也标志着乐队声音的某种转折点，尽管仍保持了支撑他们九张专辑的厚重感。

---

"'Shadowlife' opens the dreamy next chapter of Insomnium with a certain self-aware catchiness," says the band's guitarist and clean vocalist Markus Vanhala. "I set out to write an obvious hit — perhaps even a so-called 'banger' — to kick things off, but don't worry: The deeper cuts will follow. Album No. 10 is truly a defining milestone for us, so we wanted to honor our history while also introducing some new nuances. Living on the edge."

第5段："'Shadowlife'以一种自觉的朗朗上口开启了Insomnium梦幻的下一章，"乐队吉他手兼清嗓主唱Markus Vanhala说。"我着手写一首显而易见的热门曲——甚至可以说是所谓的'banger'——来开启序幕，但别担心：更深沉的作品会紧随其后。第十张专辑对我们来说确实是一个定义性的里程碑，所以我们想在致敬历史的同时引入一些新的微妙之处。活在边缘。"

---

Insomnium have several tour dates lined over the next few months and into next year, with Amorphis, Kaunis Kuolematon, and Unto Others on various dates.

第6段：Insomnium在未来几个月乃至明年有多场巡演安排，其中部分场次将与Amorphis、Kaunis Kuolematon和Unto Others同台。
