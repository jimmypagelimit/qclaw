# LIMP BIZKIT 的 WES BORLAND 说他们仍在为 SAM RIVERS 哀悼："他永远无法被替代"

**来源：** Metal Injection | **日期：** 2026-06-19 | **作者：** Cervanté Pope

---

At UK's Download Festival this past weekend, Limp Bizkit paid tribute to two recently lost members of their camp — Dougie Miller, their touring driver who passed in an accident while en route to the festival, and Sam Rivers, founding member and bassist who passed away last October.

第1段：在上周末英国Download音乐节上，Limp Bizkit向团队中最近失去的两位成员致敬——巡演司机Dougie Miller在前往音乐节途中因事故去世，以及创始成员兼贝斯手Sam Rivers去年十月离世。

---

Both were large parts of keeping Limp Bizkit going in one capacity or another, but, as the band's guitarist Wes Borland told Heavy Consequence, going on without Rivers has been incredibly difficult for all of them.

第2段：两人都以各自的方式维系着Limp Bizkit的运转，但正如乐队吉他手Wes Borland在接受Heavy Consequence采访时所说，没有Rivers的日子对他们所有人来说都无比艰难。

---

"He was like family. I'm gonna try not to cry," Borland said. "It's just like we lost a piece of our DNA. It's too hard to talk about right now in depth."

第3段："他就像家人一样。我尽量不哭出来，"Borland说。"就像我们失去了一部分DNA。现在还没法深入谈论这件事，太痛苦了。"

---

As an integral part of the nü-metal band since the beginning, performing without him has been adjustment each member is still getting used to. They've had Richie "Kid Not" Buxton on bass since November 2025, who also plays in the band Ecca Vandal with his wife, who's been doing an amazing job in the shoes he's standing in.

第4段：作为这支新金属乐队自始至终不可或缺的一员，没有他的演出是每个成员仍在适应的调整。自2025年11月起，Richie "Kid Not" Buxton接任贝斯手——他也在妻子所在的乐队Ecca Vandal中演奏——在他所站的位置上做得相当出色。

---

"He can't ever be replaced," Borland continued. "I mean, we're so lucky for Richie [Buxton], who's playing with us right now. He's such a great player, and he's just a wonderful guy, and we're gonna hold onto him for as long as we can, 'cause he also plays in a band with his wife, Ecca Vandal, and they're doing really great right now, and their career's kind of blowing up. But we're just gonna keep Richie for as long as we can and keep moving forward."

第5段："他永远无法被替代，"Borland继续说道。"我的意思是，我们太幸运了，能有Richie [Buxton]现在和我们在一起演奏。他是个出色的乐手，也是个极好的人，我们会尽可能长久地留住他，因为他也和他妻子在Ecca Vandal乐队里演奏，他们现在做得很好，事业正在起飞。但我们会尽可能长久地留住Richie，继续前行。"

---

Rivers' cause of death has yet to be determined, but a police report obtained by TMZ revealed he passed after falling in his bathroom. The report also mentioned that Rivers' had injuries consistent with a pulmonary embolism, a blocked artery in the lungs caused by blood clots.

第6段：Rivers的死因尚未确定，但TMZ获得的警方报告显示，他在浴室中摔倒后去世。报告还提到Rivers的伤情与肺栓塞一致——肺部动脉被血栓堵塞。

---

"Sam is not a replaceable person. He was the heartbeat," Borland said later on in the interview. "It's crazy talking about… very emotional talking about it. I think that last year when it happened, we were all kind of in shock, and now we're all grieving."

第7段："Sam不是一个可以被替代的人。他是心跳，"Borland在采访后段说。"谈论这些太疯了……非常情绪化。我想去年事情发生的时候，我们都有些震惊，而现在我们都在哀悼。"

---

Earlier this month, Limp Bizkit publicly released a video tribute to Rivers, which they first screened during the first show they played without him last year.

第8段：本月早些时候，Limp Bizkit公开发布了一段向Rivers致敬的视频，这段视频他们首次在去年没有Rivers的第一场演出中放映。
