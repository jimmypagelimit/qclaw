# WARTHRASH - NO LIGHT SHALL REMAIN (NCS Album Premiere and Review)

**来源:** No Clean Singing  
**链接:** https://www.nocleansinging.com/2026/06/17/an-ncs-album-premiere-and-a-review-warthrash-no-light-shall-remain/  
**评分:** 6/5  
**日期:** 2026-06-17

---

**Paragraph 1**

Metal fans who aren't familiar with the Colombian band Warthrash should be forgiven for assuming their music is straight thrash. The pointer is right there in their name, shining like a beacon on an ocean cliff. But although this band from Medellin did sink their early roots in the traditions of thrash when they started more than 20 years ago, their music has evolved in more diverse directions. Newcomers might even get a powerful sense of that by gazing upon the dark and daunting artwork by Felipe Mora that shrouds their latest album.

第1段

不了解哥伦比亚乐队Warthrash的金属乐迷，以为他们的音乐是纯正的激流金属，这是情有可原的——乐队名字就像矗立在悬崖边的灯塔，指向性太强了。虽然这支来自麦德林的乐队在20多年前起步时确实深深扎根于激流传统，但他们的音乐早已向更多元的方向发展。新乐迷甚至仅凭Felipe Mora为新专辑绘制的暗黑阴森封面，就能强烈感受到这种变化。

**Paragraph 2**

That album, aptly titled No Light Shall Remain, will be released on June 19th by the band's new label, the respected Awakening Records. It's a great display of how varied the songwriting of Warthrash has become over time, and how powerfully their performances translate those varied interests into cohesive songs.

第2段

这张专辑名为《No Light Shall Remain》，将于6月19日由厂牌Awakening Records发行——这是业界备受尊敬的厂牌。它充分展现了Warthrash多年以来歌曲写作的丰富多样，以及他们如何将这些多元风格强势转化为连贯统一的歌曲作品。

**Paragraph 3**

To be clear, Warthrash didn't cut through their thrash roots and cast them all away. This collection of 11 new songs includes a lot of fast-paced, pulse-pounding, head-hooking intensity. But, as previewed above, there's more going on here, as the band have entwined those older roots with the influence of such death metal bands as Morbid Angel, Entombed, Dismember, and of course Death itself.

第3段

必须说清楚：Warthrash并没有斩断与激流根基的联系。这11首新曲中依然充满了大量快节奏、令人血脉偾张、钩人入坑的强度段落。但正如前文所示，他们还做了一件更重要的事——将这些更早的根基与死亡金属的影响交织在一起，包括Morbid Angel、Entombed、Dismember，当然还有Death本身。

**Paragraph 4**

In a way, even the new album's introductory track "No Light Shall Remain" foretells the role that darker and more dreadful influences will play in the songs to come. It's an elegantly performed acoustic guitar instrumental with an ancient air and a mood that's melancholy, haunting, and even desolate -- a mesmerizing but unsettling experience.

第4段

某种程度上，新专辑的intro曲目"No Light Shall Remain"已经预示了接下来歌曲中更深沉、更恐怖元素将要扮演的角色。这是一首优雅演绎的原声吉他instrumental（前奏曲），散发着古老的气息，情绪忧郁、萦绕心头，甚至透着荒凉——一种迷人却令人不安的体验。

**Paragraph 5**

It flows seamlessly into "Culebras Sin Honor", and there you get an arresting sign of the band's current musical identity. The drums violently hammer; the riffing violently twitches, writhes, and blares; the guttural vocals are monstrously deep and commanding, and they reverberate as if recorded in a crypt where ghouls are awakening. The fretwork is impressively fleet-fingered, but it maniacally contorts in numerous directions as well as engaging in ravenous feeding frenzies. Even the soloing insanely shivers before ecstatically spiraling in piercing tones, and the dextrous drumwork adds to the music's exhilarating effects by constantly changing speed and rhythmic patterns.

第5段

它与下一曲"Culebras Sin Honor"无缝衔接，在这里，你首次强烈感受到乐队当下的音乐身份。鼓猛烈锤击；riff剧烈抽搐、蠕动、轰鸣；兽吼唱腔深邃如怪物般具有支配力，仿佛录音于某个食尸鬼正在苏醒的地下墓穴。指法技术上令人惊叹地飞速精准，但同时又疯狂地向多个方向扭曲，沉浸在贪婪的掠食狂潮中。solo先是癫狂地颤抖，随后以尖锐的音色狂喜地螺旋上升——精准多变的鼓技则以不断变化的速度和节奏型为音乐注入更加亢奋的效果。

**Paragraph 6**

If that song doesn't pop your eyes wide open, we'll be surprised. The following songs should keep them open wide. "Sombras del Poder" surges with turbocharged speed, the riffing swarms like carnivorous insect hordes furiously competing over gutted corpses, interspersed by short blasts of victory, and the soloing is again both crazed and glorious.

第6段

如果那首曲子还没让你大开眼界，我们会很意外。接下来的歌曲会让你继续保持目瞪口呆。"Sombras del Poder"以涡轮增压般的速度狂飙突进，riff如嗜血昆虫群落在一具具掏空的尸体上疯狂争夺，间隙穿插着短暂的胜利号角，solo再度既疯狂又辉煌。

**Paragraph 7**

By the fourth song, "Crucifixion", it becomes even more clear that the maniacal riffs of Warthrash carry melodic hooks, and that their frenzied tirades are dynamic and structured. In other words, the blazing and blizzard-like riffs continually change and return. It further becomes even more clear that the band's rhythm section are uber-talented. "Crucifixion" also includes a mood-changing interlude in which the music dismally crawls, and even the extended soloing sounds miserable and desperate; it seems to yearn for something hopeful (probably in vain).

第7段

到了第四首"Crucifixion"，Warthrash那些疯狂的riff携带旋律钩子的特质变得更加清晰，他们的狂暴宣泄是有动态和结构的——换言之，那些炽烈如暴风雪的riff不断变化、不断回归。乐队的节奏组才华横溢这一点也更加清晰了。"Crucifixion"中间还有一个情绪转换的间奏，音乐凄凉地爬行，连绵的solo听起来充满悲惨与绝望，仿佛在渴望某种希望（大概是徒劳的）。

**Paragraph 8**

Other songs following "Crucifixion" also include moments of morbid gloom and cold oppressiveness, or feverish anguish. "Undefeated", for example, includes a particularly miserable riff, tendrils of wailing melody, a soulfully grieving solo, and a dramatically majestic finale -- all of which shove big spikes into a listener's mind. It might be the album's best song for those reasons, among others. The vocals also occasionally explode from those blood-congealing roars into berserk howls, strangled snarls, or gagging grunts, but they're always frightening. We should also re-emphasize how great the soloing is, not just because it's remarkably fast and nimble (which it definitely often is) but because it's melodically founded, nuanced, and thus capable of creating differing moods as well as amazement.

第8段

"Crucifixion"之后的歌曲同样穿插着病态的阴郁与冰冷的压迫感，或者狂热的痛苦。比如"Undefeated"，就有一段格外凄凉的riff、哀号般的旋律线条、充满灵魂悲伤的solo，以及戏剧性而辉煌的结尾——所有这些都像大钉子一样扎进听者的脑海。鉴于以上种种，这首歌或许是全专最佳之一。人声也会偶尔从冰冷的咆哮喷薄而出，转变为狂暴的嗥叫、窒息的嘶吼或作呕的低吟，但始终令人胆寒。我们还要再次强调：solo太精彩了——不仅因为它极快、极灵活（确实经常如此），更因为它建立在旋律的基础上、有细微的变化，因此既能制造不同的情绪氛围，也能带来惊叹的听觉冲击。

**Paragraph 9**

Come to think of it, it's the many nuances in this album's songs that are a big part of what makes the record so successful, even though its through-line of attacking intensity is undeniable. And yes, the afore-mentioned variations in pace and mood usually occur in the midst of war-charges of head-spinning speed and ravenous voraciousness. In the main, the songs spectacularly channel the speed and intensity of big turbines capable of powering entire cities -- and the derangement of fiends freed from their hellish chains (though this madness is instrumentally crafted by the band with surgical precision).

第9段

仔细想想，正是专辑歌曲中大量的细微层次感，是这张唱片如此成功的重要组成部分，尽管它贯穿始终的攻击性强度同样不可否认。没错，前文提到的节奏与情绪变化，通常发生在战争冲锋般令人头晕目眩的速度与贪婪的掠食欲望之中。这些歌曲整体上惊人地汇聚了足以驱动整座城市的大型涡轮机的速度与强度——以及从地狱锁链中挣脱出来的恶魔的疯狂（尽管这种疯狂是乐队以手术刀般精准的器乐编排精心构建的）。

**Paragraph 10**

It really is a fantastic album, straight through to the heartbreaking and harrowing closer "Hollow Existence", and one that should hold great appeal to all lovers of death/thrash. With that, we'll leave you to it.

第10段

这真是一张不可思议的专辑，自始至终直到令人心碎而痛苦的结尾曲"Hollow Existence"，它对所有死金/激流爱好者都具有极大的吸引力。就这样，把它交给你们了。
