# Warning - Rituals of Shame Review

**来源:** Angry Metal Guy  
**链接:** https://www.angrymetalguy.com/warning-rituals-of-shame-review/  
**评分:** 6/5  
**日期:** 2026-06-17

---

**Paragraph 1**

Few luminaries in metal can suckerpunch you right in the feels quite like Patrick Walker of 40 Watt Sun and the legendary Warning. Seriously, he could be singing to you about proper chinchilla care, and many would be left a sobbing, blubbering mess as he caterwauls about what treats and bathing clay to get for your new furry little friend. If you need proof of this, go give 2006's sorrowful masterpiece (and recent Yer Metal is Olde inductee) Watching from a Distance 45 minutes of your time, and when you're done splashing cold water on your face to remove the tears and redness, you'll be hard-pressed to disagree. No one could predict that Walker would resurrect the project, as he seemed happy and content to continue with 40WS, but with its 2016 resurrection and now new album Rituals of Shame, all eyes are on Walker and crew to deliver.

第1段

金属乐坛能一拳直击你内心的传奇人物，Patrick Walker（40 Watt Sun / Warning）绝对算一个。说真的，就算他唱的是龙猫的正确饲养方法，很多人也会被他哭得稀里哗啦——却佳也不蒂。你可以先花45分钟听一下2006年的悲情杰作（也是最近Yer Metal is Olde的入选专辑）《Watching from a Distance》，洗把脸冷静之后，你大概就不会反驳了。没人能想到Walker会复活这个项目，毕竟他看起来在40WS也过得挺开心(?)挺满足的。但2016年项目重启，如今新专辑《Rituals of Shame》出炉，所有目光都聚焦在Walker和他的伙伴们身上，看他们能否再续传奇。

**Paragraph 2**

And deliver, they did. Just like its immediate and legendary predecessor, Rituals is 45 minutes of a band taking its dear, bittersweet time to wrest every milliliter of sadness out of you. From the opening seconds of the title track, Walker and 40WS bandmate Wayne Taylor smother you flat with subdued riffs and simplistic yet effective melodies, even before a single word is muttered. But once Walker begins singing, ho-boy, that familiar yearning ache returns in full depressing force. Weathered by years of life experiences, Walker's bellowing vibrato hasn't lost an ounce of effectiveness. In fact, they've become more potent over time, channeling that feeling that can only be delivered by someone who's been through various wringers and back through honest conviction. It feels like they never disbanded, only resting until the time is right.

第2段

他们做到了。《Rituals》全长45分钟，和其传奇前作一样，这支乐队用漫长的、甜蜜而苦涩的时间，从你身上榨取每一毫升的悲伤。从同名曲开篇那几秒起，Walker和40WS的队友Wayne Taylor就用低沉压抑的riff和简洁有力的人声旋律将你压得喘不过气，甚至在一个字还未唱出口之前。但一旦Walker开口唱，那种熟悉的渴望之痛便以全部的沉重感回归。历经岁月打磨，Walker那苍劲的颤音非但没有丧失一分效力，反而在时间的沉淀中愈发浓烈——它传递的，是一种只有经历过九九八十一难的人才能凭借真诚的信念传达的情感。就好像他们从未解散，只是在等待合适的时机重聚。

**Paragraph 3**

The other four songs also ensure that no light escapes or gets in. Closer "Teacher" hits with the same level of effectiveness as "Bridges" and "Footprints" did on Watching, but not taking as long to do so. Returning bassist Marcus Hatfield and new drummer Andy Prestidge (also of 40WS) added just enough variations of fills, rhythms, and flourishes, but mostly kept to a simple, heavy, and driving sound, again amplifying the anguish conveyed by Walker, his lyrics, and voice. And that's the key to the effectiveness of Rituals and Warning as a whole; you don't need flashiness, technical proficiency, or tricks and studio wizardry to be effective. Sometimes, brutally transparent honesty, wide-open space for your instruments to breathe and reverberate, and your emotions in the here and now are more than enough to get your message across.

第3段

其余四首歌同样确保没有一丝光亮能逃脱或渗入。结尾曲"Teacher"与前作中"Bridges""Footprints"有着同等强烈的感染力，但达成效果的速度更快。回归的贝斯手Marcus Hatfield和新加入的鼓手Andy Prestidge（同样是40WS成员）增添了恰到好处的填充节奏与花式变化，但整体维持了简洁、重型、驱动感十足的声音，再次将Walker的歌词和嗓音所传达的痛苦放大。这正是《Rituals》乃至Warning乐队全部作品的核心所在：不需要花哨的技巧、纯熟的演奏或录音室魔法来打动人。有时候，粗暴透明的诚实、给乐器留足呼吸回荡的空间，以及此时此刻的真情流露，就已经足够传达你的信息了。

**Paragraph 4**

It doesn't hurt that Rituals is a massive improvement in some areas in terms of mastering and production. You can hear and feel Hatfield's bass, and Prestidge's drums don't have that annoying "My Dying Bride in an empty bathroom" sound that permeated on Watching, so kudos to Tom Dring for engineering the drums perfectly. Thankfully, Chris Fullard and Adam Gonsalves left the guitars and Walker's voice alone, as what wasn't broken sure as hell didn't need fixing. The only drawback to Rituals is this isn't something I could listen to all that much. Not because of the quality, which is stellar, but it hurts to listen to on an emotional level. If you were in a good mood before listening to this, you sure as shit won't be when you're done. That said, if you need to purge and get whatever the fuck is making you sad out, I can't think of a better album to do so with.

第4段

《Rituals》在母带和制作方面的改进也是巨大的，这一点帮了大忙。你能听见并感受到Hatfield的贝斯声，Prestidge的鼓也不再带有那张前作中恼人的"My Dying Bride in an empty bathroom"的声音了——Tom Dring的鼓组工程功不可没。谢天谢地Chris Fullard和Adam Gonsalves没有去动吉他和Walker的人声，本来就没坏的东西当然不需要修。《Rituals》唯一的缺点是：这张专辑我没法反复听。不是因为质量不行，质量绝对是一流的，而是因为它在情感上太折磨人了。如果听之前心情还不错，听完之后你肯定不会好到哪去。但话说回来，如果你需要排解、需要把那堆破事带来的悲伤宣泄出来，我想不出比这更好的专辑了。

**Paragraph 5**

And that's the ... I refuse to say "appeal," but I'm at a loss for words otherwise, of Warning. You don't go into their music for gains at the gym, to romance your significant other, or to bask in someone's ability to up and down a fretboard as fast as humanly possible. You listen to Warning to reflect, to purge, and to feel. And if you're anything at all like me, you've been waiting a damn long time for a worthy follow-up to Watching from a Distance. Thankfully, Warning gave us exactly that. Welcome back.

第5段

这就是Warning的……我拒绝用"魅力"这个词，但找不到别的说法了。你不会去听他们的音乐来练肌肉、撩对象，或者去膜拜某人在琴弦上光速上下翻飞的技术。你听Warning，是为了反思、为了宣泄、为了感受。如果你跟我一样，那你就等这张堪与《Watching from a Distance》比肩的续作等了很久了。好在，Warning给了我们正是我们需要的东西。回来了。
