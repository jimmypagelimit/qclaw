# WOLFGANG VAN HALEN Says "It Was Like Closing A Book" Playing VAN HALEN Songs At TAYLOR HAWKINS' Tribute

**Source:** Metal Injection | **Date:** 2026-06-19

---

**Wolfgang Van Halen** recently spoke with **Andy Guitar** about the full circle moment it was to perform **Van Halen** songs at late **Foo Fighters**' drummer **Taylor Hawkins** tribute concert. As the son of Eddie Van Halen, he performed "Hot For Teacher," "On Fire" and "Panama" with **Dave Grohl** on bass and **Josh Freese** on drums during two Hawkins tribute shows back in September 2022, and the weight of doing so still sits with him.

"To me, it was like closing a book," **Wolfgang** said at last weekend's Download Festival in the U.K. "It was my way of being able to not only honor **Taylor**, but honor my dad by playing some of his material and just kind of keep out of the mess that is the… And so it was definitely a do-or-die thing for me."

The whole thing was fairly anxiety-inducing for him. **Hawkins**' March 2022 passing came very unexpectedly, and the six months in between it and his official tributes were not nearly enough to fully prepare for the heaviness of the situation.

"I get anxiety normally when I'm doing nothing, but for that day, my uncle Patrick will tell you, I was very… I was losing it that day," **Wolfgang** said. "To me, it was, 'If I don't do this right, if I don't serve this justice, then my life will be over.' I ended up playing it pretty all right. I still made mistakes, but it was just all live and in the heat of the moment. But it was really special. Being able to have **Dave** and **Justin** and **Josh** there supporting me, being able to play with three idols of mine supporting such a emotional and difficult thing for me to do was really special, and I don't think I could've done it without 'em… Yeah, that was a day of catharsis, for sure."

**Wolfgang** also fronts the band **Mammoth** (also known as **Mammoth WVH**), who have several festival and tour dates lined up throughout the rest of the year.
