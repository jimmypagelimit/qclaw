## 英文原文（RYM 风格定义）

Piano Rock is a form of rock music in which the piano serves as the dominant lead instrument rather than the guitar — the instrument that traditionally leads the melody and rhythm in most rock subgenres. The style emerged as early as the 1950s with the rock and roll piano of figures like Jerry Lee Lewis and Fats Domino, and later evolved through the singer-songwriter tradition of the 1970s (Elton John, Billy Joel, Carole King), the alternative rock piano anthems of the 1990s and 2000s (Ben Folds Five, Coldplay, Keane), and the indie/arena rock piano-driven sound of the 2010s and beyond (Florence + the Machine, Arctic Monkeys, Hozier).

Piano Rock ranges from intimate, singer-songwriter-oriented balladry to grandiose, anthemic rock. It often features prominent piano chords, arpeggiated piano figures, or barrel-house piano riffs at the center of arrangements. While the guitar may still be present, it typically takes a supporting role — adding texture, rhythm, or occasional solos — while the piano carries the harmonic and melodic weight.

Subgenres and related styles include Piano Pop Rock (The Fray, Augustana, Something Corporate), Piano Singer-Songwriter (Elton John, Carole King, Regina Spektor), Piano Prog (Rick Wakeman-era Yes, ELP), and Piano Indie Rock (Ben Folds, Vampire Weekend, Father John Misty).

**Representative artists:** Elton John, Billy Joel, Carole King, Queen, Ben Folds Five, Coldplay, Keane, The Fray, Florence + the Machine, Regina Spektor, Tori Amos, Something Corporate, Jack's Mannequin, Arctic Monkeys (later work), Hozier

## 中文翻译

**钢琴摇滚（Piano Rock）**是一种摇滚乐形式，其中钢琴充当主导的旋律乐器，而非吉他——吉他通常才是大多数摇滚亚流派中引领旋律与节奏的核心乐器。

这一风格最早可追溯至1950年代，以杰瑞·李·刘易斯（Jerry Lee Lewis）和胖子多米诺（Fats Domino）等人的摇滚钢琴为起点，随后经由1970年代的创作歌手传统（埃尔顿·约翰、比利·乔尔、卡萝尔·金）不断发展，1990至2000年代的另类摇滚钢琴颂歌（本·福兹五人组、酷玩乐队、基恩乐队）延续了这一脉络，2010年代以来的独立/体育场摇滚钢琴驱动之声（弗洛伦斯与机器、北极猴子、霍齐尔）则进一步拓宽了其边界。

钢琴摇滚的范围从私密内敛的创作歌手式抒情曲，到宏大壮丽的颂歌式摇滚均有涵盖。编曲通常以突出的钢琴和弦、分解钢琴伴奏或酒馆式钢琴即兴重复段为核心。虽然吉他可能仍然存在，但通常扮演辅助角色——提供织体、节奏或偶尔的独奏——而钢琴则承担和声与旋律的核心重量。

子流派及相关风格包括：**钢琴流行摇滚**（The Fray、Augustana、Something Corporate）、**钢琴创作歌手**（埃尔顿·约翰、卡萝尔·金、蕾吉娜·斯佩克托）、**钢琴前卫摇滚**（Rick Wakeman 时期的 Yes、ELP）以及**钢琴独立摇滚**（Ben Folds、Vampire Weekend、Father John Misty）。

**代表艺人：** 埃尔顿·约翰、比利·乔尔、卡萝尔·金、皇后乐队、本·福兹五人组、酷玩乐队、基恩乐队、The Fray、弗洛伦斯与机器、蕾吉娜·斯佩克托、托丽·阿莫斯、Something Corporate、Jack's Mannequin、北极猴子（后期作品）、霍齐尔
