天下熙熙，皆为利来； / The world bustles, all for profit;
天下攘攘，皆为利往。 / The world clamors, all for gain.
身有忧患兮，寒暑亡复。 / Troubled with hardship, the cold and heat of life bring no return.
生亦何欢，死亦何苦。 / What joy is there in life, what sorrow in death?
远之不逊兮，虚而委蛇； / To push the wicked out brings resentment, so one must feign indifference and yielding.
人心险恶，从来自古。 / People's hearts have been vile and foul since the distant past
登天之阶兮，世人攀附。 / The steps to heaven are climbed by the world,
如临深渊，自盲其目。 / They stumble blindly on the edge of the abyss.
幽并游侠，少小去乡。 / The northern rebels left their hometowns young.
至人潜行，东山放浪。 / The sages traveled in seclusion, roaming freely in the Eastern Mountains.
奈何狂狷兮，天砺其志。 / Alas, their recklessness was tempered by fate.
千金之子兮，不死于市。 / Privileged sons shall never be hanged in the streets
仗义每多屠狗辈， / The righteous are often from the slums,
负心多是读书郎。 / The traitors are often scholars.
花灯万里，道阻道长。 / Lanterns illuminate for ten thousand miles; the road is long and arduous.
雨来雨往，难洗荒唐。 / Rain comes and goes, but cannot wash away the absurdity.
几番鱼越兮，夜惊弦； / Several times the fish leaped, 'til the night rattles the strings
大鹏同风起，三十年。 / The bird of Da Peng is rising with the wind; thirty years have passed.
寒门再难出贵子， / It is difficult for children from poor families to rise to prominence in prosperous times,
盛世不乏结私党。 / Even in a thriving society, there's no lack of trading parties.
生而旷达乘逆旅， / Born with a free soul, I ride the journey in reverse,
踏波干云不足惜， / Treading waves and soaring through clouds with nothing to regret.
银鞍飒沓自率性， / With my silver saddle, I swiftly follow my own nature,
白云千载抚一曲。 / For the ancient white clouds, I play a melody as tribute.
