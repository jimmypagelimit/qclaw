Everyone just stood and watched me floating,
floating off the ground,
Blinking lights and hollow stares—no hands,
no hands to pull me down.
Banging air, it’s thick, it hums, it sneers,
runs cold along my spine,
Glasses clinking, voices bleeding,
calling out to me again
Everyone just passing the poison from someone to you
These words cut through the tension, leave you black and blue.
Even you hate the taste of poison, yet you stay in line
You drift between the danger and danger, bruises hollow.
Falling through the sound,
Spinning on the ground,
melted by the sun,
The sun…
