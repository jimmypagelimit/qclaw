Lainspotting Jun 02 2026
This, and the re-release, are probably one of the most important moments in my personal music and life experience. These represented a moment of realizing my own sense and journey into bisexuality, and my growing interest in rock from a place of electronic music that started as a toddler, all the way up to listening to this in middle school and developing so much. This work is just so incredibly powerful, it’s an immaculate showcase of catchy hooks, heartbreaking lyricism and instrumentals that range from ear shattering and absolutely grandiose, to even subtle and beautiful. The layers of beauty and heartbreak throughout this record are so incredibly intense, all of which feel exemplified by it’s tortured, expansive production. The ability Toledo has to switch between tender, catchy and short form compositions to these expansive and atmospheric explorations of melody, noise, and layers upon layers of his voice is unmatched. Life changing for me.

 acrogh May 30 2026
To be completely honest, so many thoughts I have on this album are inseparable from a discussion of Face to Face, it's near impossible for me to look at this album on its own when my first exposure to Twin Fantasy was the new recording.

That being said, listening to this coming off of Will's previous efforts as Car Seat Headrest, this record feels like nothing short of a miracle.

The sounds and tones and production for this album are genuinely varied incredibly well, servicing shifting song structures and different emotions across the board. The writing and vocal style perfectly portray the seething betrayal and bit of rage that Will feels, every part of this album feels so incredibly raw and vulnerable it's almost difficult to listen to.

There's a lot I appreciate in the previous albums, but this is just something else entirely. It is such an incredibly cohesive concept album that stays interesting, emotional, and high quality throughout the entire runtime.

This time when I listened to it I appreciated almost everything it did so much more than I had previously, and I still really loved this album before. This is not only a lovely piece of art, but is incredibly inspiring as a product of the creative process. It makes me want to go out and make my own thing, tell my own story, it really is remarkable. This scrappy DIY sound that is still very high effort just works for the album perfectly, I absolutely adore it.

"we were wrecks before we crashed into each other"
I can't find the words to describe my love for this album. before I really got into rock and punk-ish scenes, Twin Fantasy was my go-to. being a closeted gay teenager was really a pretty bad experience, and I got through it doing stuff I wasn't supposed to at 13. but then I found Twin Fantasy, which really encapsulates the feeling of being misunderstood and alone in a world where the outside seems like hell.
i underwent a really bad depressive episode where the only thing I felt was apathy and a lack of a will to exist, but music helped me getting through it, specifically Twin Fantasy. every single song feels like an unique experience sonically, and it formed me into the person I am today.
I will never get over this album. Thanks to Will and the band for bringing this to life. I would surely not be alive if it wasn't for this project.

TENMAVERIX May 14 2026
My Long Lost Love
A while ago I listened to car seat headrest quite casually, you know, Bodys, Fill In The Blank, Sober to Death. I've decided to return to them. I've always appreciated Twin Fantasy, I've listened to it multiple times already before this new relistening, and have always enjoyed my time. But at a point in my life where this album just fucking hits man. The things Will talks about in this album were always something I appreciated but always sort of distant from me, but now with a relisten I've realised that there is much more of me in this album then I realised. There is a feeling or emotion that Will is able to produce in this album that I can't even describe. It is depressing, and pessimistic, but in a digestible way. How? How does Will able to make sense of such extreme and messy feelings. This album is a "take one to know one". There is this feeling I get when I try to make my own music, a sense of embarrassment, a sense of disgust for your own mind, paranoia that friends or family will find it when you eventually post it and make fun of it or tell you to delete it. There is a feeling where your bent over, with your computer lagging, your back aching, your hair getting caught in your headphones, all these annoying things mixed with my current flush of inspiration and extreme feeling while I'm trying to desperately spew my feelings into this song, and it never turns out correct. Most of the time spent with this album is spent me yearning to be able to synthesize my feelings as well as Will did on this album. Feelings of being comfortable in my own skin, feelings of wanting to be free from expectations of others. My Boy (Twin Fantasy) is an awesome opener that sets my mood straight of, the Beach Life-In-Death is beautiful and epic. How does Will do this, how does will write something as somber as Sober To Death, or as simple as Stop Smoking. Nervous Young Inhumans is a song where will can talk about the process of his music, it gives an insight into his mind. This whole album I feel as if I'm in the car with this man, brushing my hair out of eyes, crying and feeling stressed over my shitty computer. I want to express myself but the limits that are placed on me are too much. But not for Will. Will can do it. Bodys is probably my favorite song. Its perfect. It rejects the idea of a clean life style being good. A self destructive, fun life style seems so romantic, getting drunk and doing drugs and having weird sex. Cute thing is a fun son, high to death is the most depressing. Famous Prophets is epic, and Twin Fantasy (Those Boys) ties the entire thing up. This album is so much to me, and I don't think I can get tired, this album already means so much to me. I love this. It has saved my youth.


I would usually put a favorite tracks part, but I just can't do that. There are albums I love better then this, but this one is so special. Please God, let me be able to write music or express myself this well in the future. I need to be free.
